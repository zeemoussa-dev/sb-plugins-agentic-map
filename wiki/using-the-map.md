## The overview

The overview shows every Section at once, each with its hub and its agents on their rings. The bar at the top counts the Sections and the agents on the map.

- **Hover** over an agent to read its description.
- **Rotate** the Sections with the arrows at the sides when the one you want is behind another.
- **The ? button** at the top left explains in short how to read the map, and links to this Guide.

Agents with no Section are drawn together on a hub called **Agents**. Each person's own Primary sits on a hub of its own (see [Who sees what](wiki:who-sees-what)).

## Focusing a Section

Click a Section's hub, or the space around it, to focus that Section full size. Its agents spread out so each one has room, and its Pipelines show as chains.

- The arrows at the edges page to the previous or next Section without going back to the overview.
- **Back to Agents Map** returns to the overview.
- Click a Pipeline's name to open its panel. Hovering over a step in that panel brings the step into view on the map.

## Finding an agent

Press **Ctrl+K**, or click the search box at the top left, and type part of an agent's name. Pick one to open its panel.

## Opening an agent or a Section

Click an agent to open its panel on the right. Once a Section is focused, click its hub again to open the Section's own panel. Both are described in [The agent and Section panels](wiki:panels).

The address keeps what is open (`?agent=…`, `?section=…`, `?pipeline=…`), so you can bookmark it or send the link to someone. The Home card links to Sections this way.

## Creating

The **+** button at the bottom right offers a new **Expert**, **Producer**, **Worker**, **Pipeline** or **Section**. Each opens the same dialog the app uses on Admin Center › Agents, so what you make here is the same as what you make there. The map redraws when you finish. Whether you may create anything depends on what your account may do.
