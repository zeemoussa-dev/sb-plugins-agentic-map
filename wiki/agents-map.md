The Agents Map draws every agent on your install as one picture. The knowledge base, your vault, sits in the middle. Around it, each Section has a hub, and that Section's agents sit on rings around it. You can see what each Section has, how the agents depend on each other, and which Pipelines run through them. From any agent you can chat with it, read what it has done, and see when it runs next.

Open it from **Agents Map** in the sidebar's Agents group, or from its card on Home.

## The three rings

Every agent has one kind, and its kind sets its ring:

| Kind | Ring | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Worker** | outside | Runs jobs on a schedule, such as capturing mail or syncing a source. |
| **Expert** | middle | Answers questions over the vault in its own area. |
| **Producer** | inside, next to the knowledge base | Keeps a document up to date and writes it back to the vault. |

Lines between agents show who works for whom: a specialist that another agent asks for help is joined to it. A Pipeline's steps are drawn as a chain, with the Pipeline's name at its start.

## What you see is yours

On an install with several people, each person's map holds only the agents they may reach. Your own Primary, if you have one, is marked as yours. See [Who sees what](wiki:who-sees-what).
