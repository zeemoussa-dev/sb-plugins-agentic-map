The Agents Map shows each person their own view of the install. It never shows you more than the rest of the app would.

## Agents by audience

Every agent is open to one of these:

- **Everyone** signed in. This is the default.
- **Admins only.**
- **Named people**, a list an Admin keeps.

The app gives the map only the agents you may see, so an agent closed to you is not on your map, in search, or counted on the Home card. Admins see every agent. An Admin who is not on a named-people agent's list still sees it, but its Chat tab says that only the named people can chat with it.

## Your Primary

Your Primary is the agent your own messages go to, in the app and from WhatsApp or Telegram.

- If you have **your own Primary**, it sits on a hub called **Your Primary**, and its panel is marked **Your Primary**.
- If an Admin **assigned you another agent**, that agent's Overview says your messages go to it.
- Otherwise your messages go to the install's Primary.

Other people's own Primaries are not on your map. An Admin sees all of them on a hub called **People's Primaries**. The Home card says which agent your messages go to.

## Audiences and vaults, for people who manage agents

If your account may manage agents, an agent's Overview adds two rows:

- **Open to**: Everyone, Admins only, or how many named people.
- **Vaults**: the vaults the agent holds, and whether it only reads them or also writes. A vault you are not a member of is counted ("1 vault you are not a member of"), never named.

Neither is changed on the map. Vault grants are set on Admin Center.
