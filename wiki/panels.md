## An agent's panel

Click an agent on the map to open its panel. It is for a quick look and a conversation. Everything about the agent itself is set on its own page.

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Its description, purpose, working mode, guardrails, vault scope, tools, who it works for and its preferred indexes. If you manage agents, it also says who the agent is open to and which vaults it holds. **Chat with…** opens the Chat tab, and **Agent definition** opens its instructions, Skills, tools and jobs. |
| **Chat** | A conversation with the agent, as in the app's chat. The activity list shows what the agent is doing while it answers, and **Show reasoning** shows its thinking. |
| **History** | Its recent sessions, each a link to the full session. |
| **Settings** | A link to the agent's own settings page. If you manage agents, it also has **Delete agent**. The install's Primary cannot be deleted here. |
| **Schedule** | The agent's jobs and when each runs. |

A note at the top of the Overview tells you when the agent is your own Primary, or the agent an Admin assigned your messages to.

Deleting an agent removes its definition, instructions and grants. Its past sessions stay in Agent Activity. An agent that another one still asks for help is not deleted, and you are told which agent asks it.

## A Section's panel

Focus a Section, then click its hub again to open its panel.

- **Overview**: its name, subtitle, description, the vault folders it covers, its fallback agent (the one that answers when nothing more specific does), and its agents.
- **Settings**: change its name, icon and colour, subtitle, description, folders and fallback agent. The pencil beside a long field opens it in a bigger editor.

The **Agents** hub and a Primaries hub are not Sections, so they have no panel.
